Materials Science & Engineering + Biomedical Engineering, Carnegie Mellon University · Class of 2029

I'm a double major in Materials Science & Engineering and Biomedical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon, by way of Seoul International School. Both fields keep asking me the same question from different directions — materials asks what makes a structure hold, biomedical engineering asks the same about tissue, bone, and the rest of the body.

Lately that's meant building 3D organ models for radiation dosimetry simulations, modeling fluid-structure interactions in the lungs and cardiovascular system alongside pediatric surgeons, and running independent research on plant-derived compounds against antibiotic-resistant pathogens — twice, on two different continents' worth of the same problem.
