About

A bit more about how I think.

I'm a student working across materials science and biomedical engineering — two fields that spend a lot of time asking the same question from different directions: what makes a structure hold, and what happens when it doesn't.

Most of what I build sits where those two overlap: a mechanical problem with a biological constraint, or a biological one that resolves once you treat it structurally. I like projects that don't fit neatly into one department.
