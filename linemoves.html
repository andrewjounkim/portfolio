Line Movement Tracker

A sportsbook posts an opening line, then stretches it as money and news come in. Pick a league to see which games the market changed its mind about, and by how much.

Data: ESPN's public scoreboard endpoint (site.api.espn.com), which carries DraftKings' odds for each game, queried live from your browser with no API key. It is ESPN's own undocumented endpoint, not an official betting-data product, so it can change or go quiet. "Open" is the first line posted; "now" is the current line. Only games that haven't started are ranked, since lines on live games aren't comparable. A fair probability is the implied probability with the sportsbook's margin (the vig) divided out, so a move in it reflects a real change in the market's opinion, not just a change in the sportsbook's cut. This is for learning how odds work, not betting advice. Bettors must be of legal age where they live. If gambling stops being fun, call 1-800-GAMBLER.
